## Overview

ATLOMY is a Multilingual Lexicon and interactive 3D atlas of ancient anatomy. It also features a documentation of re-enactments of surgical procedures as they were instructed by ancient physicians.

## Lexicon: Search and Indexes

ATLOMY is organized around Lemmas - the canonical, dictionary base form of words - that appear in ancient sources in different inflections and contexts. Every lemma has diverse meanings and uses, and ATLOMY features Lemma Pages with lexical, interpretive, and bibliographic information, as well as a searchable list of references, quotes, and distinct translations. These Lemma Pages are citable. They also connect you to 3D models in the atlas where their lemma is visualized. ATLOMY provides an easy search feature in four languages – English, Latin, Ancient Greek, and Classical Arabic.

Type a word in the search bar:

* heart / kardia / καρδία
* vessel / vein / phleps / φλέψ / عِرْق
* artery / artēria / ἀρτηρία

As you type, ATLOMY will suggest lemmas. Click on a suggestion and it will bring you to its lemma page. If a term that you are looking for doesn’t come up, or if you want to see all of the possible results, click Search or press Enter. ATLOMY will show a complete list of lemmas that are relevant to your search query.

Each index and search result page can be further filtered by Author, treatise, and system that the lemma pertain to.

Tip: As is often the case in Arabic, a single root may include several terms deriving from it. You can examine the different terms and their particular meanings by clicking on the root. This will take you to its lemma page, at the button of which appears a list of references and quotes for the different terms.

## Interactive 3D Atlas

ATLOMY’s home page features 10 3D models of different systems and layers of the human body as they are described in the ancient texts. These model pages are citable. Models are also featured on lemma pages whose lemma they feature. Clicking on a 3D model takes you to the 3D viewer. Once in the 3D viewer, use controls to navigate the model, using either your mouse of the button on the right end of the screen:

## Guides and Re-enactments

ATLOMY features re-enactments of the dissections described by the 2nd-3rd century physician Galen, in his work Anatomical Procedures. These guides contains our translation and interpretation of Galen’s writings, alongside photo & video-documentation of our re-enacted of his dissections. To enter the documentation, click on Re-enactment on the upper-right corner of ATLOMY.

Warning: The reenactment contains sensitive medical images that may be graphic. Viewer discretion is advised. Only individuals 18 years and older should watch.
