## Function

The lemma pool is the culmination point of the entire database.
It collects all entries from Ancient Word Full Reference and
classify them in lemma entries.

These lemma entries are attributed a Latin transliteration,
general English translation, and short and long descriptions.
They also collects all automated references assigned to them
in the Lemma Field of Ancient word Full references,
along with their contextual translations.

All those datasets are, upon configuration, imported to the Lemma
section of the dashboard to make new lemma pages, or to update them.
