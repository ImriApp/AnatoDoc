## Overview

Visualizations is where ancient texts are reimagined as 3D models.
When a researcher and a modeler start working on a new model,
they open a new page for it in Visualizations, where they keep resources,
track tasks, and document progress. Each model page contains subpages with:

* A link to a Google Drive folder containing the texts on which the model is based
* A table of instructions, where the researcher instructs the modeler what body parts comprise the model and how they are positioned in it. All tables of instructions are views of - and so are synched to - the central table Modelling Instructions Sub-Items (see below).
* Workflow documentation of each increment or modification done to the model
* Feedback from the researcher regarding the latest increment or modification

## Structure

### Backend tables

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Modelling Instructions Items
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists major parts or layers of the body (for example, the mouth or superficial muscles)
            and organ categories (for example, muscles discovered by Galen).
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Modelling Instructions Sub-Items
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists components of a part or layer of the body
            (for example, the tongue as part of the mouth or the biceps as part of the superficial muscles).
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Modelling Projects Overview
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists every model-building project.
        </td>
    </tr>
</table>

### Supporting tables

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            **Ancient Word Full Reference**
            *(Referenced to in Modelling Instructions Items and Modelling Instructions Sub-Items)*
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists references for Ancient words including bibliographical and attributive information about them.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            **Ancient Word Lookup Table**
            *(Deprecated in Visualizations)*
        </td>
        <td>
            In a linked relation with Ancient Word Full Reference, it lists all Ancient words in ATLOMY’s corpus.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            **Lemma Pool**
            *(Referenced to in Modelling Instructions Sub-Items)*
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists Lemmas. Each lemma is attributed a general translation,
            transliteration, short and long descriptions, list of automated
            references to the ancient texts (from Ancient Word full Reference), and more.
        </td>
    </tr>
</table>
