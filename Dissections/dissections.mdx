## Overview

Dissections is where we document our reenactments of the surgical procedures described in Galen's texts. The documentation is based on the information we have gathered from our analysis of the texts, and it includes instructions, translations, images, videos, and observations from performing the reenactments.

Dissections is linked to Ancient Dissection - Re-enacted, a seperate file on Coda which curates the data from Dissections into per-procedure dashboards. Ancient Dissection - Re-enacted is embedded in the (Re-enactment Page)[https://www.atlomy.com/re-enactment] in ATLOMY.com.

After a reenactment is performed, a new page is created for it in Dissections, with a table for documenting all steps, texts, interpretations, photos, and videos from performing the procedure.

Each reenactemnt table is built a bit differently, but they all shares some key columns:

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Step</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            The ordinal position of this step within the full sequence of steps in the procedure; unique per procedure - does not reset
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Substep</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            The ordinal position of this substep within the full sequence of substeps belonging to its parent step; resets to 1 at each new parent step
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Instruction</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            The instruction of this substep, based on our analysis of a particular passage in Galen's text
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Translation</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            Our English translation of the passage from Galen's texts, on which this substep's instruction is based
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Greek Texts</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            The original greek passage on which this substep's instruction is based
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Images</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            Still images of this substep taken at our reenactment
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Step Video Clip</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            A video of this substep as it was preformed at our reenactment (cut from the video of the entire step, which is documented in the next row)
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Full Dissection Step Timecode</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            A video of the entire step as it was performed at out reenactment, including all its substeps
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Observations</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            Any observation on this substep made by the physician, anatomist, or researcher after performing it
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            <strong>Publication Instructions</strong>
        </td>
        <td>
            Instructions and suggestions regarding how and what to include in the publication that reviews this reenactment
        </td>
    </tr>
</table>
