## Function

The Lemma Pool is the culmination point of the entire database. It collects all entries from Ancient Word Full Reference and classifies them in Lemma Entries - referred to simply as Lemmas.

Lemmas are attributed a Latin Transliteration, general English Translation, and Short and Long Descriptions. They also collect all automated references assigned to them in the Lemma Field of Ancient word Full references, along with their contextual translations.

All those datasets are, upon Configuration, imported to the Lemma section of the Admin Dashboard to make new Lemma Pages, or to update them.

## Columns

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>Lemma</td>

    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Transliteration (LA)</td>
        <td>FILL</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Translation (EN)</td>
        <td>A general English translation of the lemma, chosen according to the context of the text of ATLOMY’s corpus.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Alternative Spelling</td>
        <td>Variants of how the lemma is spelled across the ancient texts.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Source Language</td>
        <td>The language of the lemma - either Ancient Greek, Classical Arabic, or Latin.</td>
    </tr>
        <tr>
        <td>Related Terms</td>
        <td>FILL</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Short Description</td>
        <td>The introductory paragraph of the lemma to be shown at the top of its lemma page.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Long Description</td>
        <td>The deep-diving description of the lemma, its usages, appearances, meanings across contexts, and more.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>Automated References</td>
        <td>An automated list of all occurrences of the lemma in the ancient texts. This list reflects the Automated references key of rows in Ancient Word Full References that this lemma was assigned to in the lemma column.</td>
    </tr>
        <tr>
        <td>Aggregated Translations</td>
        <td>An automated list of all contextual translations of the lemma’s occurrences. This lists is the reflection   drawn from the same lines of the automated references.</td>
    </tr>
</table>
