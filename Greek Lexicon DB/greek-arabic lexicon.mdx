## Function

At the end of the modern editions of Galen's Anatomical Procedures, written by Garofalo (1986; 2000) and Simon (1906), there is a Greek-Arabic lexicon with early references. Since these translations are not context-base (i.e. they are generalized Greek-Arabic counterparts), they are not included in Ancient Word full Reference nor the Lemma Pool. Instead, they are listed in Greek-Arabic Lexicon.
