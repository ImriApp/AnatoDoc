## Purpose

Supporting Tables define recurring entities used across the Lexicon DB, including references, bibliographies, source languages, category types, and ancient words. These tables are the structural foundation for Ancient Word Full Reference and, ultimately, the Lemma Pool.

## Reference Tables

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Abbriviated Reference
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists the standardized abbreviated names of ancient texts in ATLOMY's corpus and the standardized abbreviations of their authors. Every new text must be registered here before downstream use.
        </td>
    </tr> 
</table>

## Bibliography Tables

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Early Edition Bibliographies
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists commonly used early publications of texts registered in Abbriviated Reference. Used to attribute publication metadata in Ancient Word Full Reference and to support bibliography linking on lemma pages.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Modern Edition Bibliographies
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists modern scholarly publications of texts registered in Abbriviated Reference. Used to attribute publication metadata in Ancient Word Full Reference and to support bibliography linking on lemma pages.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Special Cases 3rd Level Editions
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists additional edition layers for specific texts when standard early/modern bibliographies are insufficient.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Fragment Edition Bibliographies
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists publications of the treatise Fragments.
        </td>
    </tr>
</table>

## Ancient Word Tables

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Ancient Word Lookup Table
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists all ancient words attested in the ATLOMY corpus.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Ancient Word Full Reference
        </td>
        <td>
            Aggregates bibliographic and lexical attribution for ancient words and automatically generates references. This table is the source of automated references in the Lemma Pool and the primary structured input for lemma-page content. INCEpTION annotations are imported and transformed here.
        </td>
    </tr>
</table>

## Other Tables

<table>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Modern Terms (deprecated)
        </td>
        <td>
            Previously defined possible related terms for lemmas.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Synonyms (deprecated)
        </td>
        <td>
            Previously defined possible synonyms for lemmas. Replaced by the Synonyms field in the Lemma Pool workflow.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Source Languages
        </td>
        <td>
            Lists source languages for ancient words in the corpus.
        </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
        <td>
            Category Types
        </td>
        <td>
            Defines relevance categories for anatomy and medicine (for example, body parts, topography, action verbs, and tools).
        </td>
    </tr>
</table>

Deprecated tables are intentionally not linked to the active Lexicon DB workflow. They are retained only to preserve the definitions they contain and are not used by current downstream processes.
